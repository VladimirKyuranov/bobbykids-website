Bobby Kids
learn & play

A gentle first learning game for little ones. Children discover hundreds of colourful pictures with spoken names, then practise with simple quizzes and playful games.

  • Ages 2–8
  • No ads
  • No data collected
  • English & Bulgarian

Three ways to learn

Everything is built around everyday topics children love: animals, fruits, vegetables, food, vehicles, clothes, kitchen and home, professions, musical instruments, numbers, colours and shapes.

📖

Learn

Tap a picture and hear its name. Big, friendly cards make first words easy to explore at the child's own pace.

❓

Quiz

Find It, How Many? and Count & Collect turn new words and numbers into short, encouraging challenges.

🧩

Play

Memory Game, Sort Them, Match the Shadow and Where Is the Sun? build attention, logic and visual skills.

How unlocking works
