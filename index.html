Three ways to learn

Everything is built around everyday topics children love: animals, fruits, vegetables, food, vehicles, clothes, kitchen and home, professions, musical instruments, numbers, colours and shapes.

📖

Learn

Tap a picture and hear its name. Big, friendly cards make first words easy to explore at the child's own pace.

❓

Quiz

Find Me, Count Me and Collect & Count turn new words and numbers into short, encouraging challenges.

🧩

Play

Memory, Sort Them, Match the Shadow and Where Is the Sun? build attention, logic and visual skills.

For parents

Made to be a calm, safe space for small children.

  • No advertising and no tracking.
  • No accounts, no chat, no links out of the app.

  • Levels grow gently in difficulty as your child plays.
  • Free to try. A one-time purchase unlocks all content, with no subscriptions.
  • Purchases and settings sit behind a parent check.
