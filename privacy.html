Bobby Kids – Privacy Policy

Effective date: 10 November 2026

Bobby Kids is an educational game for young children, made by Vladimir Kyuranov, publishing as Eldergrove Myths ("we", "us"). We care about children's privacy. This policy explains what happens to information when you use the app.

The short version

Bobby Kids does not collect, store on our servers, or share any personal information. There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking.

Information we collect

None. The app does not ask for a name, email, location, photos, contacts or any other personal information, and it does not send information about you or your child to us or to any third party.

Information stored on the device

To work properly, the app saves a few things only on your device: game progress (such as unlocked levels), settings (such as language and sound) and whether the full version has been purchased. This information never leaves the device and is removed when you uninstall the app.

In-app purchase

The app offers a one-time purchase that unlocks all content. Purchases are only possible after an adult answers a parent check. Payments are processed entirely by Google Play; we never see or receive payment details. Google's handling of purchase information is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

Advertising and third parties

The app shows no advertising and contains no third-party analytics or advertising services. There are no links to social networks or external websites for children.

Children's privacy

Bobby Kids is designed for children aged 8 and under and follows the Google Play Families Policy. Because we do not collect personal information, we do not collect personal information from children, in line with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR).

Changes to this policy

If we change how the app handles information, we will update this page and the effective date above.

Contact

If you have any questions about this policy, please contact us at eldergrove_myths@abv.bg.
