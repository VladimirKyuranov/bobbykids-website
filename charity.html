20% for sick children

I have made a promise to myself: 20% of the income from Bobby Kids will go to charity, to help sick children whose families cannot afford their treatment.

This page is where I keep that promise in the open. Every donation is listed below, with proof.

How it works

🪙

20% of the income

Every time a family buys the full version, the app store first takes its fee and the taxes. Of the revenue that reaches Eldergrove Myths, 20% is set aside for donation.

🩺

For children's treatment

The money goes to help sick children whose families cannot pay for the treatment they need.

🧾

Proof for every donation

Each donation is added here with its date, amount, recipient and a receipt or bank confirmation.
